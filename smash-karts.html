About Smash Karts

Smash Karts is a free multiplayer kart racing arena game. The goal of the game is to collect surprise boxes and survive to the end.
Survive and fight using whatever you find: bullets, grenades, and rockets.
Smash Karts is an amazing world where you drive fast, shoot rockets and create massive, eye-popping explosions.

Some power-ups in Smash Karts

Missiles: These can be used to fire guided missiles at your opponents, causing damage to their karts.
Speed Boosts: These power-ups can be used to give your kart a temporary speed boost, allowing you to overtake your opponents.
Shields: These power-ups can be used to protect your kart from incoming attacks.
Mines: These can be dropped behind your kart to damage any opponents that run through them.
Invincibility: This power-up makes your kart invincible for a short period of time.

How to play

Choose a kart and customize it to your liking.
Create a private room or join an online game.
Use the arrow keys or WASD to navigate the arenas.
Collect the power-ups scattered across the map.
Use the spacebar to activate acquired power-ups.
